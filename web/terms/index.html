Short version

Ambient Field Lens is a research prototype. It never tells you it is safe to proceed: every verdict is verify, and there is no setting that changes that. Read the passage it shows you, in your own manual, before you touch a machine. It is not a safety device.

Scope

The app was built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon and is demonstrated on a single instrumented machine. It may be distributed as a development or TestFlight build. Features, the hosted endpoint and the documentation corpus can change, break or be withdrawn at any time.

What it does, and what it will not do

  • It measures whether your documentation covers the question, shows the passage it retrieved, and refuses when nothing is close enough.
  • It never emits a green step and never instructs you to act on the machine. The verdict is always verify and the step is always empty.
  • It does not command machinery. The arm in the demonstration moves only on a human command, over a local link the hosted service cannot reach.
  • Segmentation masks are candidates, never proof that an object is present and never an approval to proceed.
  • The evidence card binds which frame, question, passage and masks produced an answer — but not when the photograph was taken. A picture from yesterday passes every check on the card.

Safety

Do not use Ambient Field Lens as a safety device, an interlock, a guard, a lockout procedure or a substitute for the machine's own documentation, its manufacturer's instructions, or a qualified person. It is not certified for safety-critical use and carries no such certification. You remain responsible for the machine, for the people near it and for deciding whether any answer applies to your situation.

AI outputs

  • Answers are produced by a language model over retrieved passages and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong.
  • The coverage gate is a measured distance between your question and your documents. It is a measure of proximity, not of relevance and not of correctness. A question can sit close to the corpus and still be absurd.
  • A refusal means the documentation does not cover the question. It is not a claim that the machine is unsafe, nor that the question has no answer.

Your documentation

If manuals or other documents are loaded as the retrieval corpus, you confirm you have the right to use them for that purpose. The corpus is versioned and the original is preserved, but the app is not a backup and should not be the only copy of anything you need.

Permitted use

  • Do not use the app to create unlawful, harmful, abusive or rights-infringing content.
  • Do not attempt to defeat the refusal behaviour, the coverage gate or the call budget, and do not reuse or share the single-use call tokens that cap GPU spend.
  • Do not misuse the gap-report channel, which is read by a person.
  • Do not reverse engineer restricted distribution components, except where applicable law or an open-source licence permits it.

Availability

The service is provided on an as-is and as-available basis. The GPU window is budget-capped and may be closed, after which requests fail rather than degrade — the system is designed to stop rather than guess.
